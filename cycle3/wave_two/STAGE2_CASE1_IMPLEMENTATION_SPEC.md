## 4. Budget, forecast, floors, and precision (components 2.4 / 2.5 / 2.8 instantiation)

**Joint budget structure (NAMED L2 RULING ITEM under the amendment's 2.4 independence clause):** the joint speaking probability is computed as P(evaluable) x P(recover | evaluable), each estimated by end-to-end replicates, with the dependency handled by conditioning rather than assumed independence:

- **EVALUABILITY replicates (R_geom):** each replicate simulates the FULL frozen design matrix (180 CM-1 + 135 CM-0 rows) with a fresh calibration seed-set, then evaluates through the frozen pipeline the complete geometry-bearing stack: G1 (incl. the three-component CM-0 dominance on that replicate's own CM-0 rows, computed and frozen before its CM-1 rows are processed, mirroring the read's cm0-first discipline), G4 strict-all, the band filter, and the FULL G3 machinery - global bin grid, N_bin_min/S_bin_min, half-single-seed rule, phase rule - over the complete frozen comparison universe, Amendment-1 path-(a) bin qualification included. Replicate-level outcome: eligible-family counts per path, per axis-relevant geometry. EVALUABILITY PROBABILITY (per path) = fraction of replicates with >= 1 eligible family on that path. No permutation machinery needed (geometry is permutation-free), keeping R_geom computationally honest.
- **RECOVERY replicates (R_rec):** on replicates SAMPLED FROM THE SAME DISTRIBUTION of evaluable geometries produced by the evaluability replicates (L2 required precision, folded: never a handpicked easy eligible subset; the sampling rule is uniform over the evaluable draws of the evaluability arm), the positive-control cells (P1/P2/P3 per Section 3) run the FULL detection machinery - 199 permutations per window per axis, raw + residue floors, k_onset/s_onset, and for P2 the tracking burden inside the replicate's eligible bins. RECOVERY PROBABILITY = fraction recovering at the target claim tier (predeclared lower rungs recorded as shortfalls, per the amendment). **If evaluable draws are too rare to estimate recovery honestly (the precision rule unattainable within the exhausted ladder), the result is recorded as an EVALUABILITY failure of the reconstructed design - never a license to force idealized geometry.**
- Method statement: Monte Carlo over end-to-end synthetic replicates; uncertainty via binomial confidence intervals (Wilson, 90% level) on each probability; the joint number reported with propagated interval. This structure is submitted to L2 for explicit acceptance as genuinely joint (not per-gate reasoning re-labeled): the geometry replicates carry every inter-gate dependency including the Amendment-1 coupling; the conditioning replaces, rather than assumes away, the evaluability/recovery dependency.

**Reconstructed design floors (frozen here; L2 attack surface per the amendment's 2.5):**
- **Seedability (evaluability) floor: P(>= 1 eligible path-(b) family) >= 0.5 AND P(path (a) evaluable) >= 0.5, where "path (a) evaluable" is defined in the SAME UNITS as path (b) (L2 required clarification, folded): at least one G3-eligible, bin-qualified path-(a) evidentiary family under Amendment 1 - never mere availability of the global setting flag.** Rationale: the historical audit's "FEASIBLE" verdict asserted the design could speak; the weakest quantitative reading a seeding decision can carry is more-likely-than-not on each falsification path. A design below even that is not seedable without an explicit severity defense. (The floor is deliberately permissive - it makes an S1 hit HARDER, not easier: the machinery must find the design below even this weak floor.)
- **Recovery floor: P(P1 recovery at target tier | evaluable) >= 0.8.** Rationale: P1 realizes the committed mechanism at committed magnitude through the detection arm alone; a frozen pipeline that recovers the substrate's own committed effect less than 4 times in 5 is insensitive by its own anchors.
- **Forecast interval level: 90% predictive intervals** (Wilson for probabilities; empirical 5th-95th replicate quantiles, method="lower", for geometry counts: eligible bins per path, windows per bin per side, seed counts). Interval construction method reported per quantity; no post-hoc widening; the non-vacuity rule of mini-contract S3(a) applies.
- **Forecast content (2.5):** per (tier, c-label) cell: predicted window_mean_rho strata under drive (Engine-A realized, not mean-field-only; the construction-only mean-field bands computed alongside as the approximation-disclosure exhibit, with the addendum's fixed-point form rho* = p_eff/(1-s+p_eff) named as the historical audit's approximation and its blind spot - within-cell temporal stratification under the stepped schedule - stated); predicted strata separation vs Delta_rho_match + buffer (0.00432); eligible-bin count distributions per path; windows/bin/side and seed-count distributions; claim-tier reach those numbers support.

**Precision rule (mini-contract D3, instantiated):** R_geom >= 100 and R_rec >= 100 minimums. For every probability entering a floor decision, the 90% interval half-width must be <= 0.5 x |point estimate - floor| (the predeclared fraction: one-half the decision margin).
