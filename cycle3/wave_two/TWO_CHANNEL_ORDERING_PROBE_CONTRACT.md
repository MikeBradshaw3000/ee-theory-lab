## 3. Waveform, schedule, and the primary analysis family

**Form: stepped three-level block ramp.** One cycle = 3 blocks = 75 ticks, levels [0, u/2, u] in fixed order, repeating; block-constant on the canonical 25-tick cadence; phase fixed with cycle start at block 0; frozen before any run; identical at every cell and across all seeds. Three-level structure chosen for identifiability: three distinct plateaus per cycle, and a 3-block period cannot be produced by any harmonic of the 2-block ring.

**Period: 75 ticks / 3 blocks.** Cadence-aliasing constraint satisfied numerically against the committed scripts (Section 5): NOT 25/1-block (block cadence); NOT 50/2-block (the committed Rule E saturate/extinct ring signature — sharpest exclusion); NOT 100/4-block (equal to WINDOW_LENGTH; integer cycles per window alias to a per-window DC offset, defeating G1 at window aggregation); NOT 200 or 400 ticks (one-or-fewer cycles gives no repeat structure).

**Schedule-imprint statistic (L2 Amendment 3, agreed):** G1 never rests on Pearson correlation alone. The primary statistic is the phase-locked regression of block-mean rho on the centered known schedule over the primary analysis blocks, reporting both the signed slope in rho units and the Pearson correlation. A row passes driver tracking only if the slope is positive, the correlation exceeds the fixed threshold, and the slope/covariance amplitude exceeds the fixed threshold. A high correlation with negligible amplitude is recorded as no driver imprint.

**Primary phase-balanced analysis set (L2 Amendment 2 + C.5, agreed):** The primary block analysis set is blocks 3–14 inclusive: four complete 3-block cycles. The primary window analysis set is windows whose starts are blocks 3–11 inclusive, so every 4-block window lies wholly inside the primary block set and the three schedule-start phases are represented equally (three windows per phase; starts 0–12 would give 5/4/4, unbalanced). Blocks 0–2, block 15, and all non-primary windows remain recorded diagnostics only. This exclusion is for complete-cycle and phase-balance discipline, NOT because the construction assumes slow dynamical relaxation (Section 1 lag convention: relaxation is effectively instantaneous). No G1–G4 pass, onset claim, or earned-window claim may be based on an incomplete-cycle or phase-imbalanced window set.

**Amplitude tiers: u ∈ {0.10, 0.25, 0.50} logits; u_max = 0.50; plus the u = 0 reference tier.** Time-mean lift per tier = u/2 at the logit level (see Section 5 static-offset matching for the rho-level distinction). Measurability anchor, primary-source-cited: committed block-scale sigma_M at the Lambda = 0.40 coupled baseline is 0.0017–0.0025, and sigma_M IS block-rho sigma — c3_w2_rule_e_bounded.py lines 331–335 construct baseline_block_means from block-mean rho and take np.std(..., ddof=0) over them; same scale as the claimed swing. The smallest tier's within-cycle mean-field rho swing (~0.398 → ~0.414 between the 0-block and the u-block at u = 0.10) is ~6 sigma_M. Calibration discipline (binding): tier amplitudes are calibrated on rho-lift and non-degeneracy ALONE (Comparator-0 discipline); the observables are outputs, never tuning targets. Fence 5 applies to the driver: a tier whose realized analysis-block rho exits [0.35, 0.55] or fails G4 is fenced as an apparatus limit, not retuned against observables mid-probe.

The thresholding stage emits:

4. **G3 rho-match scale:** a fixed absolute Δrho_match or rho-bin width derived from committed block-rho variability (sigma_M scale, Section 3). IQR intersection alone is not a threshold.
5. **G3 bin-eligibility constants (C.4 agreed text):** N_bin_min (minimum primary windows per side per bin) and S_bin_min (minimum represented seeds per side per bin), emitted before any CM-1 or CM-0 output exists. ## 7. Gates and onset (agreed replacement text)

**G1 — schedule imprint, hard gate for nonzero CM-1 tiers.** Row-level; applies only to nonzero schedule tiers. On the primary analysis blocks, regress block-mean rho on the centered frozen schedule at the predeclared lag-0 phase convention. Report the signed slope, covariance amplitude, Pearson correlation, block-rho variance, and schedule phase. A nonzero CM-1 row passes G1 only if: (i) the phase-locked slope is positive; (ii) the correlation exceeds the thresholding-stage floor-imprint threshold; (iii) the slope/covariance amplitude exceeds the floor-amplitude threshold; and (iv) the same statistic exceeds the matched-c, matched-tier CM-0 comparator distribution under the predeclared dominance rule. Correlation without amplitude is no driver imprint. Overdispersion without phase-locked schedule response is no driver imprint. No G1 pass means no common-mode-lift claim; the row is an apparatus limit. The u = 0 tier is excluded from G1.

**G2 — differential reach at the propensity level.** Tier-level for the kappa sweep, row-level for activation reporting. The operative activation floor at |c| = 0.35 is fixed by the thresholding stage from committed Lambda = 0.40, |c| = 0.35 prop_I lower-quantile behavior, with only the predeclared G4 slope attenuation allowance. Tiny monotone increments from zero do not pass G2. A sweep passes G2 only if prop_I rises with |c| under the predeclared trend rule (unit of evaluation, fixed here and L2-accepted at the fidelity pass: per nonzero tier, per sign, across the seed set, under the thresholding-stage aggregation rule; signs are reported separately before any sign-symmetry summary is made) and the |c| = 0.35 rows clear the committed-anchor floor for both signs, or else the sign failure is explicitly reported. If G2 fails, the probe FAILED TO ACTIVATE the differential channel; no ordering failure is readable from that region.

**G3 — matched-rho / common-support comparison.** Pairwise-comparison-level, not row-level. Two settings enter an ordering comparison only inside predeclared rho common-support bins. A valid matched-rho comparison requires: (i) overlapping primary-analysis block rho support; (ii) absolute mean difference within the thresholding-stage Δrho_match; (iii) phase-balanced representation of the schedule levels or schedule-window phases inside the compared samples; and (iv) at least N_bin_min primary windows and S_bin_min represented seeds on each side inside the same predeclared rho bin and phase-balanced analysis family. Bins failing the count rule are reported as common-support failures, not as ordering evidence; the count rule cannot be relaxed after seeing probe output. IQR intersection plus one pooled SD is reported as a descriptive overlap diagnostic only. The stratified read is primary: organization reported as a function of BOTH rho and prop_I; no kappa-tracking claim off an un-stratified sweep.

**G4 — compression / slope audit.** Row-level and block-level. For every primary analysis block, compute the full cellwise becoming-active probability field and report: mean p_become, mean cellwise logistic slope mean_i[p_i(1 − p_i)], lower-tail slope quantile, and the fraction of cells outside the predeclared non-compressed probability interval. A row is in-band only if all thresholding-stage slope and tail criteria pass. The proxy p̄(1 − p̄) is reported only as a summary; it is never sufficient, because it can hide compressed high/low cell tails under an innocuous mean.

**Onset criteria — axis-specific and paired (L2 Amendment 13, agreed).** Onset is evaluated separately for Psi_meanI_state and Psi_persistence_I; no pooled observable and no theoretical Psi is introduced. A setting-level paired ordering claim must report both axes. A common-mode-alone failure at kappa ≈ 0 may occur on EITHER co-equal axis if that axis shows raw effect above its predeclared floor threshold and control-surviving residue above the same-axis threshold. Requiring both axes to light up before recording a common-mode-alone failure is PROHIBITED. For each axis, onset requires jointly: (a) raw effect above the thresholding-stage threshold for the same primary window family; (b) per-cell independent time-shuffle residue (defined in Section 6.7) above the same-axis floor-residue threshold; (c) z-score reported alongside but never sufficient. The setting-level flag uses the standing earned-window rule only after that rule has been verified for the primary number of windows (Section 5 window-opportunity discipline).
