## 0. Purpose and the governing distinction

The program's false-positive protection is architecturally sound and is not modified by one word of this amendment: thresholds frozen before output, no rerun, no relaxation after output, candidate language, fail-closed verification, Mike as sole arbiter. What the TCOP arc exposed is a distinct, unpriced risk on the other side:

**FALSE-POSITIVE PROTECTION IS ABOUT WHEN RULES ARE FIXED; UNDER-DETERMINATION PROTECTION IS ABOUT WHAT THOSE RULES ARE CALIBRATED AGAINST.**

Freezing rules early protects against tuning-toward-a-result. It does not protect against freezing a rule stack that no realizable dataset could ever satisfy. The fix is upstream design validation, never downstream mercy. Two principles are this amendment's spine, carried verbatim:

- "A test that returns under-determined regardless of what the substrate does is not a test." (L1)
- "A test that only detects the synthetic effect it was secretly shaped around is also not a test." (L2)

The first principle motivates the audit; the second constrains it. The audit must demonstrate that the frozen instrument CAN speak, without the demonstration itself becoming a hidden design preference that shapes the instrument toward one anticipated effect.

**Scope of the protection (L2-required, verbatim intent):** Passing the evaluability audit is not a guarantee that the probe will speak. It is a pre-seed showing that non-speaking is not already baked into the frozen design under the committed synthetic and forecast assumptions. The audit does not eliminate under-determination; it makes it pre-priced, attributable, and less likely. It requires the design to state and test why it is expected to speak.

Worked motivating instance (TCOP, committed record): the feasibility audit computed common-support feasibility from construction-only mean-field bands and answered "could matched-rho bins exist." It was never required to compute (i) the joint probability that the stacked gates (strict-all G1 dominance; conjunctive raw-AND-residue per-window onset at max-observed floors; k_onset=2; s_onset=3; strict all-primary-block G4; Delta_rho_match=0.00283 with N_bin_min=9 / S_bin_min=3; lexicographic tracking burden; bin-qualified path (a)) would permit ANY outcome to register; (ii) whether the frozen pipeline would recover a committed-magnitude synthetic effect injected into the floor family; or (iii) a falsifiable forecast of realized eligible-bin counts under drive. The realized census - 76 out-of-support / 6 count-failed / 4 phase-confounded / 0 eligible - was the predeclared contingency occurring, unpriced.

**On the prior audit (L2 replacement text, D3 merge):** the prior feasibility audit correctly reported a construction-level necessary condition under the then-current protocol; it did not issue - and was not asked to issue - a quantitative forecast of realized read geometry or joint gate passability. The TCOP result shows that a construction-only admissibility statement was not enough to price realized evaluability risk. This amendment upgrades future pre-seed feasibility from a qualitative scope warning to a quantitative evaluability forecast requirement. The prior audit is not thereby recharacterized as defective; it met the standard then in force.

## 1. Scope and standing

This amendment modifies the DESIGN-CONTRACT TEMPLATE for future probes. Every future probe's design contract must carry a PRE-SEED EVALUABILITY section with the eight components of Section 2, completed and frozen BEFORE seeding, on the same commit discipline as every other contract element.

**Outcome taxonomy for the audit itself (L2-required; binding throughout):** the audit's possible verdicts on a design are three, and they never blur:
- **INVALID EVALUABILITY SECTION** - the audit's own instruments (controls, budget computation, forecast) are incomplete, uncomputable, unscorable, or leak-prone; the evaluability section must be redone; no verdict on the design is issued.
- **NOT SEEDABLE** - the audit's instruments are valid and the design fails them; redesign required; routes to Mike like a not_feasible feasibility halt.
- **SEEDABLE-BUT-SEVERE** - the audit's instruments are valid, the design passes its halt floors, and the reported speaking probability is low by deliberate severity choice; seedable only with the severity explicitly defended and Mike's acceptance recorded as a severity choice.

This amendment does NOT:
- reopen, re-read, revise, or supplement the TCOP result, which stands permanently as recorded (under-determined on both co-equal axes; apparatus limits, not evidence; the ordering hypothesis open);
- apply retroactively to any completed probe - retroactively applied rules cannot issue findings;
- decide the attenuation-allowance design question or the matched-rho geometry design question (both on Mike's desk, named-not-triggered; the Stage 2 calibration may inform them, never decide them);
- verify the 9-window earned-window rule, reclassify Comparator 0, move L4, or touch CM-2, Rule E B/C, Lambda=0.20, or the no-density-stability discipline;

## 2. The pre-seed evaluability section: eight required components

Structure: L2's eight-component skeleton, adopted; priority lineage L1 proposals 1/2/4 with L2's insertion of 3 before 4. All positive and negative controls are evaluated against the design's TARGET CLAIM TIER (2.1), never against a universal candidate-production tier (L2-required; prevents over-triggering on deliberately descriptive or deliberately severe designs).

### 2.1 Target claim tier

State what the run is supposed to be CAPABLE of establishing: the strongest claim the design is built to reach (e.g., a risky-form produces / fails-to-produce outcome on named axes), stated as a claim tier, not a hoped-for result. Every downstream component is evaluated against this tier. A design whose evaluability section cannot name its target tier has no target and is not seedable.

### 2.2 Positive controls

Predeclared synthetic effects of COMMITTED plausible magnitude, injected into null/floor-family data, which the frozen pipeline MUST recover. Requirements:

- Magnitudes ground in COMMITTED anchors only (for the current program's scale: the prop_I ~ +0.38 propensity anchor; committed raw Moran scales ~0.004-0.011 meanI / ~0.05-0.07 persistence; Reading-D's measured attenuation fractions ~1-3% meanI / ~13-18% persistence). "Plausible" means: the magnitude the committed record says the substrate produces when the mechanism operates - never a magnitude chosen to be recoverable.
- **Full-pipeline traversal (L2 Blocker 3, verbatim intent):** positive controls must traverse the FULL frozen pipeline, including eligibility geometry, binning, matching, aggregation, and claim-tier gates. A positive control that demonstrates effect detection while bypassing the comparison geometry does not satisfy this component for designs whose claim tier depends on that geometry. (The TCOP failure was evaluability geometry, not detection; a control that skips the geometry validates the wrong thing.)
- **Morphology diversity (L2-required):** where the target claim admits multiple plausible morphologies (spatial texture, temporal profile, axis loading), the positive-control set must include AT LEAST TWO distinct morphologies, of which at least one is expected to be easy for the pipeline and at least one plausible morphology expected to be difficult. Validating only the easiest anticipated morphology is a hidden design preference. Where the design claims one morphology exhausts the target, that claim must be stated and defended - and that defense is L2 attack surface (Section 3).
- Injection substrate is committed null/floor data only. No responsive-run data enters a positive control.
- Recovery criterion: the frozen pipeline, run unmodified on the injected data, registers the effect at the target claim tier (or at a predeclared lower rung, stated in advance with the reason). Recovery at a weaker tier than targeted is a recorded shortfall, not a pass.
- **Control validity precedes control verdicts (L2-required; D2 symmetric):** a positive-control failure is binding only after the positive control itself has survived L2 morphology and magnitude review as a valid control. If L2 rejects the injected effect as non-representative, too easy, too hard, or theory-misaligned, the outcome is INVALID EVALUABILITY SECTION (Section 1 taxonomy), not a non-seedable design.

### 2.3 Negative controls

Predeclared wrong-shape and null injections the frozen pipeline MUST reject. Sensitivity alone is not validation; the audit demonstrates sensitivity AND discrimination. Minimum content: at least one pure-null injection and at least one structured-but-wrong-shape injection per axis the target claim engages. Requirements:

- **Rejection standard (L2 replacement text):** for deterministic finite controls, the pipeline must stay silent. For repeated stochastic controls, the pipeline must remain below a PREDECLARED false-positive tolerance appropriate to the target claim tier. A correctly calibrated severe pipeline can carry a finite false-positive rate; the tolerance is frozen with the section, never fitted afterward.
- **Wrong-shape discipline (L2-required):** a wrong-shape control must be wrong by the TARGET CLAIM'S own predeclared morphology, not merely by being a shape the current pipeline is unlikely to detect. Negative controls chosen to be trivially rejected validate nothing.
- **Control validity precedes control verdicts (D2):** a negative-control false pass is binding only after the negative control itself has survived L2 review as a valid wrong-shape or null object. If the injection is wrong only under L1's interpretation but admissible under the theory's, pipeline firing may be correct; the outcome is INVALID EVALUABILITY SECTION, not a non-seedable design.

### 2.4 Compound conservatism budget

Enumerate EVERY conservative gate choice in the frozen stack - floors and their branch (e.g., max-observed vs. percentile), strict-all conjunction rules, dominance components, count minima (k/s aggregation, N_bin/S_bin), bin widths and cap bindings, seed requirements, phase rules, qualification rules (e.g., bin-qualified failure paths) - and compute the estimated JOINT probability that the instrument speaks at all under the Section 2.2 positive controls. Requirements:

- **Two quantities, separately reported (L2-required, verbatim intent):**
  1. **EVALUABILITY PROBABILITY** - the probability the instrument yields an admissible read at the target claim tier rather than an under-determined / non-speaking outcome;
  2. **RECOVERY PROBABILITY** - the probability the instrument recovers the positive-control effect at the intended tier, CONDITIONAL on evaluability.
  A probe can be evaluable but insensitive, or sensitive in principle but non-evaluable because bins collapse; TCOP's failure was primarily evaluability geometry. The two numbers never merge.
- **Joint computation defined (L2 replacement text):** the joint speaking probability must be computed by passing COMPLETE SYNTHETIC REPLICATES through the frozen pipeline END-TO-END, including all dependencies among gates, bins, windows, seeds, phase rules, and aggregation rules. A product of marginal gate pass rates does NOT satisfy this requirement unless L2 explicitly accepts the independence assumptions as justified for that design. (The TCOP instance shows why: Amendment 1's bin-qualification routed BOTH falsification paths through the same matched-rho bin geometry, so the bin census emptying constrained path (a) as well as path (b). Per-gate reasoning cannot see that coupling.)
- **Method and uncertainty discipline (L2 Blocker 4):** the budget must state its estimation method - analytic derivation, Monte Carlo over injected controls, bootstrap over floor-family data, or another named method - and report uncertainty or sensitivity bounds where the estimate is simulation-based or model-based. A joint number without method and uncertainty is false precision and does not satisfy this component.
- NO universal cutoff. Some instruments are legitimately severe; a hard-to-pass gate can be exactly right. The requirement is that the joint numbers be COMPUTED, FROZEN, and REPORTED - never left implicit in a stack of individually defensible choices. (The thresholding addendum's recorded G1 apparatus limit - "hard-to-pass by design, coarseness known" - is the existing qualitative practice this component makes quantitative and joint.)
- L2 receives the formal attack: "is this instrument likely to speak at all - and is the joint computation actually joint, or per-gate reasoning re-labeled?"
- Sufficiency of the reported numbers is MIKE'S CALL. The budget informs the seeding decision; it never makes it. A deliberately low speaking probability accepted by Mike is recorded as a severity choice (SEEDABLE-BUT-SEVERE, Section 1 taxonomy).

### 2.5 Evaluability forecast

A quantitative, falsifiable forecast of the realized geometry the read will face: expected eligible bins, windows per bin per side, seed-level counts, bracketable comparisons, and the claim-tier reach those numbers support. The forecast must be stated in the same units the read's eligibility rules use, so that the run TESTS the forecast. Requirements:

- **Uncertainty structure (L2-required):** forecasts must include uncertainty bands or scenario ranges wherever the geometry is stochastic or model-estimated. The realized read is scored against the predeclared forecast RANGE, not only against a single expected value - avoiding false calibration failures where the forecast was probabilistic all along.
- **Approximation disclosure (L2-required):** if the forecast depends on mean-field or construction-only approximations, the approximations and their expected failure modes must be NAMED in the section. This converts "necessary-not-sufficient" from a scope disclaimer into a structured forecast with declared blind spots.
- "Forecast 14 eligible bins, realized 0" (against the predeclared range) is a recorded CALIBRATION FAILURE of the feasibility model - attributable, entered in the record, feeding the next design - never a shrug. A forecast the realized data cannot score is not a forecast and does not satisfy this component; it is an INVALID EVALUABILITY SECTION condition (Section 2.8).
- The forecast must commit to realized-geometry numbers INCLUDING under any drive or conditioning the design applies (the TCOP instance: drive-induced rho-stratification was the binding constraint; a forecast in realized units would have had to confront it before seeding).
- **The design's halt floor is attack surface (L2-required):** the evaluability floor the design sets for itself (Section 2.8) is itself L2 attack surface. A floor set so low that the instrument is allowed to seed while likely non-speaking must be explicitly defended, and Mike's acceptance must be recorded as a severity choice.

### 2.6 Optional calibration tranche

Permitted ONLY when a scale literally cannot exist pre-run - when no committed data can supply the geometry a rule needs. Named CALIBRATION TRANCHE, deliberately - never "pilot," which connotes a rehearsal of the outcome. Binding rules:

- Allowed outputs are NAMED before the tranche runs; conversion formulas are FROZEN before the tranche runs.
- **Geometry-only variable discipline (L2 replacement text):** the tranche may inspect ONLY geometry variables needed to instantiate the read - support widths, bin occupancy, variance scale, schedule amplitude, and the like. It may NOT inspect target observables, candidate flags, near-null flags, signed-regime flags, raw effect sizes used in the claim tier, or any diagnostic that would reveal whether the target pattern is present.
- **Pre-run leak analysis (L2-required):** every calibration tranche must include a pre-run leak analysis explaining why the named scale variables cannot function as target-outcome proxies under any reading. If a named scale variable is itself plausibly outcome-bearing, L2 must explicitly review it and either approve it as geometry-only or reject the tranche design; material entanglement with candidate flags, substantive observables, or claim-tier outcomes makes the tranche inadmissible without Mike-opened redesign.
- **Formulaic summaries (L2-required):** tranche outputs are converted by the frozen formulas alone; tranche summaries are formulaic, never manually interpreted.
- **Unusable-output halt (L2-required):** if the frozen conversion formula returns an unusable or non-seedable design, the tranche HALTS. It does not authorize manual adjustment, a second tranche, or formula revision without a separate Mike-opened arc. ("The pilot told us the right scale, but we tweaked the formula" is the named leak this closes.)
- Tranche data is EXCLUDED from all confirmatory statistics. No tranche-adjust-tranche loop without a separate Mike-opened arc.

Principle: real data may estimate GEOMETRY, never choose SUCCESS CRITERIA.

### 2.7 Claim ladder

Predeclared lower-rung finding tiers, each with its own FROZEN thresholds, fixed before seeding: apparatus observation / measurement-scale finding / scoped negative / candidate pattern / risky-form outcome (naming adapted per design; the rungs and their thresholds are the requirement, not this exact list). The 97.5th-percentile floors traveling alongside TCOP's operative max-observed floors are the existing instance of a waiting, pre-specified second rung; this component generalizes that pattern to the whole claim structure.

Anti-salvage guard, verbatim: **"Missing the top rung does not authorize inventing a lower rung. It only activates lower rungs that were already specified."**

- **Activation freeze (L2-required, both passes folded):** a lower rung is active only if its THRESHOLD, EVIDENCE OBJECT, and ALLOWED WORDING were frozen before seed, and only if its required data were collected under thresholds and diagnostics frozen before seeding. A missing top rung cannot justify computing new diagnostics, alternate floors, or alternate aggregation rules after output. The claim ladder is never a post-hoc alternative-analysis menu, and "apparatus observation" prose is never a catch-all rung.

### 2.8 Halt rules and audit verdicts (D1: L2's six conditions, organized under L2's three-category taxonomy)

**Design-level halts - NOT SEEDABLE without redesign; route to Mike like a not_feasible feasibility halt; each binds only after the relevant controls have survived L2 validity review (control-validity-first, D2):**
1. A positive control required for the target claim tier is unrecoverable by the frozen pipeline.
2. A negative control passes at the target claim tier.

**Evaluability-section-invalid conditions - the AUDIT is incomplete or defective; the evaluability section must be redone before any verdict on the design is issued; no seeding decision is licensed either way:**
3. The compound conservatism budget cannot be computed as a genuinely joint probability over the frozen gate stack (Section 2.4 method discipline unmet).
4. The evaluability forecast cannot be scored in the same realized units the read will use.
5. An optional calibration tranche would inspect variables that can function as outcome proxies for the target claim (leak analysis failed or absent).

**Currency condition - EXECUTION BLOCKED pending Section 4 recomputation:**
6. A pre-execution amendment changes the gate stack or comparison universe and the affected evaluability components have not been recomputed and re-reported.

**Floor-breach halt:** independently of the above, the evaluability forecast falling below the design's OWN stated floor (the floor is the design's to set and freeze; it is L2 attack surface per Section 2.5; the halt binds against it) renders the design NOT SEEDABLE without redesign - unless Mike, with the floor's defense on record, accepts the severity explicitly (SEEDABLE-BUT-SEVERE, recorded as a severity choice).

A halt here is a design outcome, not a failure of anyone's work: the audit did its job before a seed was spent.

## 3. Mandatory L2 attack surface

The evaluability section is itself attack material, and the following questions are written into the template as REQUIRED L2 attack surface - the anti-comfort-theater guard. An evaluability section that has not survived these is not complete:

- Why THIS synthetic magnitude? (Provenance to committed anchors, or the injection is shaped around what the pipeline can find.)
- Why THIS morphology set? Does it include a plausible difficult morphology, or only the easiest anticipated one? Would a different plausible morphology fail recovery? (If yes and unaddressed, the positive control is a hidden design preference.)
- Are the negative controls wrong by the target claim's own predeclared morphology, or merely shapes the pipeline is unlikely to detect?
- Is this validating the intended THEORY test, or an easy toy that shares its surface form?
- Does every positive control traverse the full eligibility / binning / matching / aggregation geometry, or does any bypass it?
- For the conservatism budget: is this instrument likely to speak at all - and is the joint computation actually joint (end-to-end replicates with stated method and uncertainty), or per-gate reasoning re-labeled?
- For the forecast: is the design's halt floor set low enough to evade the halt rule, and if so, is the severity defended?
- For any calibration tranche: could the named scale variables leak outcome information under any reading (the pre-run leak analysis is the object under attack)?

## 4. Evaluability-package currency under pre-execution amendment (L2 replacement framing folded; principle L1's, wording L2's)

If any gate rule, comparison universe, binning rule, floor, aggregation rule, or qualification rule is amended after the evaluability section is frozen but before execution, and the amendment is independently justified by a NAMED BLOCKER or design correction (TCOP's Amendment 1 is the motivating instance), then EVERY AFFECTED evaluability component must be recomputed and re-reported before execution proceeds - positive controls, negative controls, compound conservatism budget, evaluability forecast, claim-ladder reach, and halt-rule status, wherever affected.

Binding constraints:
- The recomputation is a CURRENCY UPDATE ONLY. It licenses nothing and relaxes nothing; it merely restores currency after the frozen stack changes.
- It may NOT be used to choose among alternative gate rules or to tune the rule stack toward an acceptable budget. Amendments are blocker-driven, never budget-seeking.
- If the recomputed budget or forecast falls below the design's frozen floor, the design halts or routes to Mike as a new design revision (Section 2.8).
- Any FURTHER amendment after recomputation invalidates the prior evaluability section and requires a fresh freeze. There is no amend-recompute-amend loop.
