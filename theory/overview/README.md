## What this directory is

The citable statement of the theory of entrepreneurial ecosystem emergence at the mean-field architecture level. When a document in this repository, a review packet, or a manuscript needs to say what the theory *says*, it cites the current version here.

## What it is not

- **Not a contract input.** Flight contracts, the frozen specification (`flights/mfa_fidelity/spec/`), the gate records and the instrument are governed by their own documents and identities. Nothing in the Overview binds a flight; nothing in a flight is a claim about the Overview except through a registered bridge (see `flights/mfa_fidelity/rulings/MFP_REGISTRATION_v1_1.md` and the notation-scope ruling).
- **Not under the TDA firewall.** The Theory Development Annex (`tda/`) is exploratory and quarantined from review packets (TDA-000 §3). The Overview is the opposite: it is what the Annex refines toward, and it may be cited.
- **Not Phil-facing routing.** Mike manages all Phil-facing routing; a copy here is a repository fact, not a delivery.
