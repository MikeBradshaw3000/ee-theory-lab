# Ruling Record — N3 Permanent Branch: `become_survive` Supports the Q-Disabled Subset Only
**Ruled by:** Mike Bradshaw, Theory Architect, 2026-08-25. **Recorded by:** L1 (third succession); ratified by Mike at placement.
**Companion:** N3_ARBITRATION_BRIEF.md (carries both options in full, including the ontological analysis added at Mike's scrutiny before ruling). **Jurisdiction:** instrument implementation subset; the frozen spec (§1.3 permissive) is unamended.

## Ruling

Option B. The construction-time refusal of nonzero-Q `become_survive` configurations is the **permanent supported subset**: `become_survive` runs Q-disabled configurations only, and refuses loudly otherwise. The refusal states non-implementation and non-jurisdiction, never non-meaningfulness. TelemetryWriter B-field integration (Phase-2 item 4) proceeds against this subset.

## Grounds (summary; the brief is the full record)

Option A would mint kinds the theory does not license — causally orphaned state and a severed-loop Q — and make Psi_local's standing configuration-dependent, in exchange for structural parallelism and a diagnostic largely obtainable measurement-side. Phenomenology outranks formalism; the misinterpretation surface (passenger bases read as mechanism) is a phenomenology-side cost purchased for a formalism-side benefit.

## Preserved cautions — the against-side, stored for full revisitability

1. **Permanent skeleton asymmetry.** The two rule modes now diverge structurally forever within this ruling. Future maintenance of the common skeleton must not assume symmetry; a refactor could silently weaken the refusal. Standing mitigation: the refusal is construction-time and loud, and pinned by test; any change to it is a change to this ruling.
2. **Deferred cost lands on successors.** If a future contract needs live Q under B-comparable dynamics, it pays full implementation plus review then, without amortization, likely under later L1s. This ruling accepts that placement of cost deliberately.
3. **The offline substitute is weaker than a live operator in one specific way.** Telemetry-side reconstruction recovers Q's *single-step* response to B-generated fields exactly (Psi_local and is_active are emitted). It does **not** recover the *compounding* passenger trajectory — Q iterating on its own prior writes — without building an offline Q-iterator. That iterator is buildable measurement-side and mints no ontology, but it is real work, not free reading. The brief's "available without the ontological cost" is qualified to this extent, of record.
4. **Foreclosed serendipity.** No one will see live base trajectories under B dynamics unless this ruling is deliberately reopened; an unanticipated signature of Q's response characteristics in that stream, if one exists, stays unobserved.

## Reopen conditions (named, not triggered)

This ruling is fully revisitable. Any of the following routes a new arbitration to Mike with this record and the brief as mandatory inputs:
(a) a registered contract requiring coupled or hybrid dynamics on the B rule (e.g., post-E4 exploration wanting B-comparable dynamics with live terrain) — noting that a base-*reading* B rule is a new rule mode and spec-amendment territory beyond Option A itself;
(b) evidence that the skeleton asymmetry is breeding drift defects in practice;
(c) a registered phenomenological question about Q's compounding response to B-generated activity fields that the offline iterator (caution 3) is shown insufficient to answer.

## Option-space note (Mike's observation at ruling)

The binarity is real and diagnostic: the question as repaired by L2's N3 is type-level — what does construction do with a nonzero-Q `become_survive` configuration — and type-level questions admit exactly accept or refuse. The wider space (hybrid rule modes reading bases into the B logit; partial Q; offline iterators) consists of *different questions*: new rule modes are spec-amendment territory, and measurement-side tools are not constructor behavior at all. That the fork presented as clean binary is evidence the N3 repair carved the joint correctly.
