## The rulings

**PD1 — All three bases, simultaneously.** An action stream participates when, within the window, the stream's actions register on viability (v) and on social reinforcement (r), with transition cost (c) below its prohibitive limit, all evaluated at the stream's own situational configuration of the bases. If any base sits at its annihilating extreme (v = 0, r = 0, c = 1), the stream is invisible to the aggregate variables regardless of the other two. *(Wording per Mike's 2026-10-10 correction: "the stream's actions register on v and r," not "registers non-zero on v and r." Registration is an event of the stream's actions; v and r are registered by events; c < 1 is a standing condition.)*

**PD2 — Attainable and realized projection.** The terrain determines the region of projection attainable from a situation; orientation is realized — or not — as projection within that region. Favorable conditions permit participation without producing it: the theory records that a stream did not project and requires no account of why. Orientation and projection are one articulation seen from the stream's side and from the conditions' side (Overview §4, §5).

**PD3 — Constituency-as-denominator rejected.** Streams registering on some bases but not all are heterogeneity within the picture, not a second population with theoretical standing. The aggregate variables are defined over participating streams within the relevant domain. The April 2026 "partial projectors feeding Q" reasoning rests on an architecture violation — Q reads only ρ and Ψ — and is rejected.

**PD4 — Nearness to the participation boundary.** Nearness is |Λ − Λ*| evaluated at the situational configuration. *(Retained unchanged at PD7; Mike explicitly did not want PD4 changed.)*

**PD5 — Window-based simultaneity.** "Simultaneously" in PD1 means within a window W. W is an architectural parameter — a declared timescale on which participation is defined — not a tuning choice. *(A pending amendment, held by Mike 2026-10-10 and not yet made: W's existence is committed and its value is the instrument's declaration, the same status as Λ*; "architectural parameter" as written invites a search for the true W. Until Mike modifies this ruling, the text above stands.)*

**PD6 — χ-operationalization reserved.** The operationalization of registration (χ) — the formal rule by which particular observed action streams are identified as registering, from data collected under other descriptions — is the central instance of the empirical normalization problem, a measurement-theory question reserved for dedicated work. No flight contract may assume an operationalization this ruling has not supplied.

**PD7 — The boundary is the activation surface; registration is an event.** The participation boundary is the activation surface Λ = Λ*. The annihilating faces (v = 0, r = 0, c = 1) are where registration is impossible and are the surface's limiting case — a stream on any face has Λ = 0 — so the two conditions are entailed, not two boundaries. Registration (χ) is a realized, stochastic event, not a position in the space of structural conditions: below the surface registrations occur and decay; above it they persist. PD4 is unchanged.

## The layering (ratified with PD7)

Four questions, kept apart because collapsing them produces the misreading that participation is a position:

1. **Attainability** — is registration possible? A question about the stream's situation: yes wherever the coordinates sit off the annihilating faces.
2. **Registration** — has an action registered? A question about what the stream does, answered by what occurs; conditions permit registration, they do not perform it. (v and r are registered by events; c < 1 is a standing condition.)
3. **Participation** — do registrations add up? A question about the window W (PD5).
4. **Persistence** — does participation keep happening? A question about the terrain: below Λ* registrations lapse, above it they are sustained (PD7).

The density variable ρ counts the third; the first transition is a statement about the fourth. **A stream participates; a region activates.** Activation is a property of a domain — participation density sustained by coupling among streams — and the theory has no such thing as an activated agent (Overview v1.8 §5, §10).
