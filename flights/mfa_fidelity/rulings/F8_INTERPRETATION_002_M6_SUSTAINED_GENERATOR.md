## The sentence and why it needs an interpretation

§5.1 names three regime sources for the audit generator: the Markov null (NULL), "the realization's own rule at the level's m" (SUSTAINED), and the DIP construction (UNRESOLVED). Read literally, the SUSTAINED source is the §2 lattice: a 50×50 synchronous Moore run under the hazard rule at η₀. An M6 audit draws up to 12 classes × 500 sweeps × (24 + up to 8) levels × 20 seeds of such runs per ensemble (≈ 3.84 M runs as an upper bound; ≈ 1,400 serial hours at the timing L1 measured and L2 confirmed arithmetically but did not re-time), and placed M1's run-spec guard (`f8/config.py::f8_run_spec`) refuses production-shape runs at pass-2 insertion levels and off the declared panels. The literal reading cannot execute within the stage. L1 proposed reading A — independent two-state chains with the hazard η₀ + gλ̄·ρ*(m; η₀), started at ρ* — and described it as "the contract's own closure with g restored". L2 showed that description to be incomplete: A freezes the feedback at the equilibrium density and starts at ρ*, whereas the §3.1 closure feeds back the *current* density and starts at π₁; at m = 1 the two relax with factors 0.7736 (A) and 0.9122 (the §3.1 map's derivative at ρ*). L1 verified both figures and withdraws the "contract's own closure" description of A. L2 recommended reading E; Mike ruled E.

## The interpretation (binding)

1. **For the M6 generator's SUSTAINED regime only, the per-cell local density d_i(t) of §2.3 is replaced by the current global density K_t/N, and the run is the aggregate count process:**
   K₀ ~ Binomial(N, π₁), π₁ = a/(a + q) the §2.9 null's stationary density, a = 1 − e^(−η₀Δt), q = 1 − e^(−μΔt);
   K_{t+1} = U_t + V_t, with U_t | K_t ~ Binomial(K_t, 1 − q) and V_t | K_t ~ Binomial(N − K_t, a_m(K_t)) independent given K_t, where a_m(k) = 1 − e^(−(η₀ + gλ̄·k/N)Δt) and gλ̄Δt = m·q is the level relation of §2.8.
   This is the construction placed M5 already implements in `f8/closure.py::finite_n_counts` (`9452dd11396350ab…`) with its default stationary-null start. Its conditional expectation is exactly the §3.1 map: E[K_{t+1}/N | K_t/N = x] = F_m(x). The generator is therefore the **finite-N, current-density closure of §3.1 as a stochastic count process**, started as the committed realization is started (§4.1, stationary null).
2. **What E is and is not.** E is a well-mixed count closure: cell transitions are conditionally independent given the count and their paths are coupled through the evolving count. It is not the §2 lattice (no spatial correlation), not reading A (feedback is not frozen at equilibrium; the start is π₁, not ρ*), and not a sequence of independent per-tick draws (the Markov temporal dependence is retained). Its finite-N stationary mean is not exactly ρ*(m; η₀): F_m is concave, so stationary fluctuations shift the mean below the deterministic fixed point; this is a property of the declared stochastic closure and is never corrected by resetting, truncating or conditioning samples. With the feedback coefficient set to zero and the same start, E reduces in distribution to the §2.9 Markov null, which is M6's NULL source; the M6 implementation of that source declares its own draw order and stream separation (it is not byte-identical to M1's one-uniform-per-cell stream and claims no M1 trajectory identity). The loose "N INDEPENDENT cells" wording in `finite_n_counts`'s docstring is superseded by this paragraph; no M5 source change is made.
3. **Modulations (`persistent_nonstationary_high_m`, `nonmonotone_amplitude`) act on the synthetic feedback coefficient, not on the fixed-g canonical rule.** The generator declaration states a target density r_tar(m, t) = π₁ + κ(m, t)·[ρ*(m; η₀) − π₁] with κ's exact formula, parameter distributions, time origin, onset convention and phase; converts it in two steps — activation probability a_tar = q·r_tar/(1 − r_tar), then hazard H_tar = −ln(1 − a_tar)/Δt — and runs the count process of point 1 with a_t(k) = 1 − e^(−(η₀ + c_tar(m, t)·k/N)Δt), c_tar = (H_tar − η₀)/r_tar, which equals gλ̄ at the unmodulated ρ*(m; η₀). Legal domain, enforced at parameter preflight: π₁ ≤ r_tar < 1/(1 + q) (a finite, non-negative hazard that preserves the spontaneous floor); an illegal draw is refused and redrawn under the declared bounded redraw rule, never clipped. A moving target is a target, not the realized mean, and is never asserted to be one. The preflight examines the **resulting** above-onset profile (a decreasing κ does not by itself give a decreasing amplitude, because ρ*(m; η₀) rises with m) and the actual presence of the declared drift or oscillation over the observation schedule; E1's geometric `archetype_preflight` is a pattern, not a proof of these conditions.
4. **Sampling is never conditioned on classifier outcome.** Regime assignment is per sweep by the common per-seed uniforms §5.1 prescribes; those uniforms are shared across levels, stochastic trajectories are not. No NULL, SUSTAINED or DIP path is redrawn because M2 returned an unintended status; the run-status errors that produces are the errors the audit exists to observe, and their effect on labels and routing stays inside the frozen applicable-error accounting. Parameter-level preflight certifies legal rates, target margins and the named feature; it certifies nothing about any individual trajectory's status.
5. **The DIP (UNRESOLVED) construction is adapted to F8's single terminal window.** Under placed M2 (`42daeb4663c5783f…`) S_term is the tenth window alone, so E1's `gen_dip_run`, which may zero window 9, would produce S_min = S_term = 0 → NO_SUSTAINED, not UNRESOLVED, and §6.4's "U deterministically" would fail. The F8 DIP is a declared count-grid construction satisfying **S_min ≤ θ_P and S_term > θ_T by its support**: a zeroed nonterminal window and a terminal window whose count support lies strictly above θ_T·(WINDOW·N) on the grid, specified before sampling, confined to the DIP regime (never a post-classification repair of a SUSTAINED trace). Build evidence exercises terminal placement and threshold equality explicitly.
6. **Insertion levels are scored through an identified M6 reachable-level adapter.** `f8/null.py::null_thresholds` and `build_threshold_cache` (`4ee3f239b27f772b…`) accept pass-1 levels only, and the §4.3 law is level-independent. M6 binds the router's reachable micro-unit surface (from `f8/verdict.py` `c79ce0d7c81e21d5…` `route()` post-fallback over the canonical band witnesses, as E1's `reachable_pass2_levels` did) to the one certified null payload identity, with completeness and identity established before any audit and a missing reachable key failing closed as an apparatus halt. The adapter does not promote a slice cache, does not relabel an off-grid object as an M3 object, and does not weaken M1's run-spec guard or M3's level domain; any change to a placed module would be its own disclosure and L2 round.
7. **`no_null_consistent_anywhere` means SUSTAINED-regime assignment at every level** (the realization "never decays" in §5.2's gloss). At positive floor the empty state is not absorbing and not unreachable; the wording creates no zero-truncation and no resampling rule, and does not promise that every run receives SUSTAINED.
