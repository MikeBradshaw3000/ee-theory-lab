# 1. Question 1 — Is NOT PRODUCED foreordained under the frozen rule and null?

## RULING: **CONCUR WITH A NARROWER STATEMENT; DO NOT REGISTER THE EXACT VERDICT AS A THEOREM YET**

Three propositions must be separated.

## 1.1 What the rule’s form does establish

For the frozen interaction term,

\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{feedback}}(d)
=
\beta d-\delta d^2
=
d(3-4d),
\]

the interaction drive exceeds the no-density-term null drive whenever

\[
0<d<0.75.
\]

The floor makes zero density non-generic, and every sampled closure equilibrium lies well inside that positive-feedback interval.

The frozen synchronous rule also does not have a distinct survival/deactivation channel. Each cell’s next state is redrawn from the same activation probability irrespective of its present state. An active cell can turn off with probability \(1-p_{\mathrm{act}}\), so it is imprecise to say that deactivation is literally absent. What is absent is a **separate state-conditioned survival/deactivation process whose balance with activation can change stability**.

Therefore the frozen rule cannot instantiate the proposed “activation overcomes explicit decay” mechanism. It may still exhibit nonlinear collective changes through its density map, but it cannot test the particular persistence transition now intended for P1.

A further structural point follows from the present null construction. The null retains terrain drive and the floor but removes the density terms. When the floor sustains \(d>0\) and the feedback increment is positive, the interacting mean is generically displaced above the null mean. The null threshold margin is a finite-\(N\), finite-window quantity. As resolution increases, that margin contracts while a genuine mean displacement persists. Thus any E1 onset under this rule is fundamentally a **resolution-dependent detection boundary**, not automatically a bifurcation or change in dynamical structure.

That conclusion is architectural and does not depend on the 701-point job.

## 1.2 What is not yet a theorem

The structural argument alone does not prove that:

- every one of the 701 closure levels is `S`;
- no higher level becomes `N` or `U`;
- the reference class is exactly `NO-NULL-PHASE`; or
- the spatial stochastic lattice returns `NOT_PRODUCED / no_null_consistent_phase`.

The current evidence makes those outcomes strongly expected:

- the hard-floor point \(m=0.15\) clears \(\theta_P\) by a large certified margin;
- every sampled level is `S`;
- the sampled fixed-point census is single-valued;
- the lift grows strongly with \(m\); and
- projection-side sweep 0 gives 20/20 sustained runs at the hard floor and all-S pass 1.

But the dense reference record and lattice record remain results, not deductions merely from memorylessness.

A proof could potentially replace the dense search if it established, under certified bounds:

1. the closure orbit remains in a region where \(G_m\) has the required monotonicity;
2. the orbit/fixed point is nondecreasing in \(m\);
3. the certified S-margin at the hard floor is positive; and
4. the projected-tail statistic preserves the order.

No such proof is presently registered. The 701-point job should still run.

## 1.3 Exact answer

The correct statement for the record is:

> The frozen rule is structurally unable to test a transition defined as activation overcoming an explicit state-conditioned decay process. Its E1 line, if any, is a null-relative finite-resolution detection boundary. `NO-NULL-PHASE` is the strongly expected reference result, but the exact dense-grid and lattice verdicts remain canonical results to be produced.

Production therefore still answers a nontrivial question:

> Does the stream-level spatial realization recover the closure’s adverse structure?

It does not answer:

# 4. Question 4 — Flight 8 minimal rule

## RULING: **THE FOUR-PART IDEA IS SOUND, BUT THE CONTRACT NEEDS A MORE EXACT DYNAMICAL SPECIFICATION**

The proposal names the right missing ingredients:

- state memory;
- explicit deactivation;
- local-conduciveness-gated feedback;
- retained spontaneous floor;
- and a null formed by removing feedback alone.

Several additions are required before contract drafting.

## 4.1 Specify two state-conditioned transition probabilities

Let \(x_i(t)\in\{0,1\}\), local conduciveness \(\lambda_i=F(v_i,u_i,r_i)\), and normalized local active density \(d_i(t)\).

A clean hazard-based candidate is:

\[
P[x_i(t+1)=1\mid x_i(t)=0]
=
1-\exp\{-[h+g\,\lambda_i d_i(t)]\Delta t\},
\]

\[
P[x_i(t+1)=0\mid x_i(t)=1]
=
1-\exp(-\mu\Delta t).
\]

Here:

- \(h\) is the spontaneous-action floor;
- \(g\) is endogenous feedback gain;
- \(\mu\) is the deactivation rate.

This bounded form avoids probabilities exceeding one and makes the memory channel explicit.

The symbols are illustrative. Do **not** reuse the current \(\beta\) for deactivation; it already names a different coupling coefficient in the frozen substrate.

Other transition forms are admissible, but the contract must freeze the exact discrete-time rule before asserting a normal form.

## 4.2 Derive the discrete-time crossing, not only an ODE slogan

The homogeneous mean-field map for the hazard candidate is:

\[
\rho_{t+1}
=
(1-\rho_t)
\left[1-e^{-(h+g\bar\lambda\rho_t)\Delta t}\right]
+
\rho_t e^{-\mu\Delta t}.
\]

At \(h=0\), its multiplier at \(\rho=0\) is

\[
J_0=e^{-\mu\Delta t}+g\bar\lambda\Delta t
\]

to first order in the feedback hazard. The discrete-time stability crossing is \(J_0=1\), or

\[
g\bar\lambda\Delta t=1-e^{-\mu\Delta t},
\]

which reduces to \(g\bar\lambda\approx\mu\) only in the small-\(\Delta t\) limit.

The contract must pre-register the exact discrete-time prediction. The continuous-time transcritical equation is the normal-form limit, not the substrate equation.

## 4.3 A positive floor removes the exact transcritical bifurcation

With \(h>0\), \(\rho=0\) is not an equilibrium. The transcritical crossing is unfolded into a rounded or avoided transition.

Therefore Flight 8 needs two explicitly separated objects:

1. **zero-floor structural skeleton** \(h=0\), where the closure can have an exact transcritical crossing; and
2. **committed positive-floor realization** \(h>0\), where the contract tests the predeclared imperfect unfolding.

Retaining the floor in the physical realization is compatible with using \(h=0\) as the structural comparator. It is not legitimate to claim an exact transcritical crossing in the positive-floor system.

## 4.4 All terrain dependence relevant to the crossing must be explicit

If a terrain-only activation drive remains when \(d=0\), then the exact absorbing-state/transcritical structure is lost even at \(h=0\).

To obtain the proposed threshold \(g\Lambda\approx\mu\), either:

- all local-conduciveness dependence enters through the feedback channel; or
- the baseline terrain term is retained and the actual linearization, not the simple slogan, is used.

The null \(g=0\) must retain:

- the same floor;
- the same deactivation;
- the same initialization;
- the same update timing;
- the same bases;
- the same exogenous-drive state;
- and every other parameter.

Only endogenous feedback is removed.

## 4.5 Keep local and projection-level quantities distinct

The lattice rule should read local \(\lambda_i\), never an aggregate theorist-level \(\Lambda\).

Under a homogeneous mean-field projection, the threshold may involve \(E[\lambda]\). On the heterogeneous lattice, linear stability is generally controlled by the leading eigenvalue of a weighted neighborhood operator such as

\[
g\,\mathrm{diag}(\lambda_i)A,
\]

not merely by \(gE[\lambda]\).

The contract should therefore pre-register:

- the projection-level bracket; and
- the lattice-level spectral or finite-size prediction,

as related but distinct objects.

## 4.6 Isolate the primary mechanism

For the primary P1 flight:

- bases should be frozen;
- Q should be disabled;
- exogenous drive should be zero;
- optional noise should be off;
- update timing should be fixed;
- topology and normalization should be frozen.

Otherwise a moving slow channel can shift the proposed crossing during the measurement.

Low- and high-activity initializations should be included as a diagnostic. A transcritical branch should not display the hysteresis expected from a saddle-node mechanism.

## 4.7 Define P1 structurally

The primary Flight 8 endpoint should be:

> a fixed-point/stability change in the zero-floor closure at the pre-registered Jacobian crossing, with the positive-floor realization showing the predicted rounded continuation.

A null-relative detection boundary may remain a secondary finite-design observable. It must not again become the definition of P1.

## 4.8 Markov-null redesign

Under \(g=0\), each cell is an independent two-state Markov chain with transition matrix

\[
P_i=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-a_i & a_i\\
q_i & 1-q_i
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The exact null must freeze:

- initial distribution or stationary initialization;
- burn-in, if any;
- transition timing;
- whether \(a_i\) and \(q_i\) depend on local bases.

Per-tick marginals are not enough. Temporal sums are correlated.

For one cell, a window-occupancy generating function can be computed by a two-state transfer matrix. Across cells, the total window count is a convolution of independent Markov-binomial contributions. But the ten window totals are jointly correlated, so \(S_{\min}\) requires either:

- a certified joint transfer-matrix/dynamic-programming construction;
- a prospectively conservative familywise bound; or
- a separately qualified simulation-based threshold with explicit finite-sample coverage.

The existing M3 convolution of independent ticks cannot be reused.

# 5. Question 5 — §10 normal form and notation

## RULING: **THE TRANSCritical/LOGISTIC REWRITE IS A §15-SANCTIONED WORKING-HYPOTHESIS REVISION, SUBJECT TO EXPLICIT CONDITIONS**

The current cubic form,

\[
\dot\rho=a(\Lambda-\Lambda^*)\rho-b\rho^3+\eta(t),
\]

is pitchfork-like. For a bounded nonnegative activity density, no \(\rho\mapsto-\rho\) symmetry has been stated. Without such a symmetry, a quadratic nonlinear term is generically allowed and should not be omitted merely to retain Landau form.

If the zero-floor dynamics preserve \(\rho=0\), the linear coefficient crosses zero, and the quadratic coefficient is nonzero with the stabilizing sign, then the natural local normal form is:

\[
\dot\rho=(g\Lambda-\mu)\rho-c\rho^2.
\]

That is the appropriate transcritical/logistic candidate.

This is not automatic solely because \(\rho\ge0\). The revision should state and derive the conditions:

1. zero activity is invariant when the floor is zero;
2. one control parameter changes the linear stability;
3. the quadratic coefficient is nonzero;
4. no symmetry forbids it.

The exact closure expansion should establish those coefficients.

## 5.1 Positive floor as an imperfection field

A more mechanistic bounded form is:

\[
\dot\rho
=
(1-\rho)\,[h+g\Lambda\rho]
-
\mu\rho
+
\xi_\rho(t).
\]

At \(h=0\), the transcritical skeleton is explicit. At \(h>0\), the transition is rounded.

The theory should say “imperfect transcritical unfolding” for the positive-floor system, not “exact transcritical crossing.”

## 5.2 §15 status

The Overview already places the supercritical form at committed-working-hypothesis level and allows it to fail while deeper architecture survives.

Replacing the cubic activity normal form after a registered mismatch is exactly that sanctioned correction, provided it is:

- versioned;
- prospectively registered;
- distinguished from hard-core commitments; and
- not used to reinterpret prior data silently.

This is not a hard-core failure.

## 5.3 Cubic term for Ψ

Retaining the cubic for \(\Psi\) is justified only if \(\Psi\) is a signed order parameter with an identified symmetry or another derivation suppresses the quadratic term.

If \(\Psi\) is a nonnegative magnitude, the same objection applies. The cubic should not be retained merely because it has moved from \(\rho\) to \(\Psi\).

## 5.4 Resolve the eta collision

Reserve:

- `η_floor` for the deterministic spontaneous-action floor.

Use:

- \(\xi_\rho(t)\) or \(\zeta_\rho(t)\) for stochastic forcing, with its law and covariance stated; or
- \(h(t)\) if the term is an external deterministic field.

The current unlabelled \(\eta(t)\) is unacceptable because it is both semantically ambiguous and symbolically collides with `η_floor`.

## 5.5 Third-order comparison

Holding the lattice-versus-ODE expansion is correct. Its target cannot be fixed until:

- the floor/noise distinction is settled;
- the discrete-to-continuous bridge is stated; and
