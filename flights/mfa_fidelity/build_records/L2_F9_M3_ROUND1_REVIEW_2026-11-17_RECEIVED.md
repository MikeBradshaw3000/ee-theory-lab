## Q2 — Pruning

**No counterexample established for the contract's decision.** For n bonds, let k=floor(19(n−1)/20). If the global E→C quantile is at most ε, at least k+1 bonds have distance at most ε. For each such bond e, the crossing cells of the nearby contour bond are at most ε+1/2 from e. Their comparison values straddle the contour level, so `min ω < ℓ ≤ max ω` holds for at least k+1 bonds. The `_intervals` construction enumerates exactly the integer doubled-coordinate disc of radius 2ε+1=5, and `admissible` counts these intervals. Consequently a contour capable of S≤ε cannot be pruned. When no compatible contour exists, a pruned descriptive minimum need not be the full-family minimum, as §5.5 step 7 expressly allows. This is a proof for the decision, not a claim that all descriptive adverse minima are exact.

## Q6 — Defect D1: unsigned multiplicity sum overflow

**Location:** `mfa_instrument/f9/evaluator.py`, `check_weights`.

The validation computes `w.sum()` in the input's unsigned dtype. The sum can wrap modulo 2^64 to the expected 40 even though the actual mathematical sum is vastly larger. Casting afterward to `int64` creates negative weights. The function thus accepts values contradicting its own documented nonnegative multiplicity invariant. A constructed-resample matrix containing such a row can pass `evaluate_level`'s row validation and propagate an invalid map rather than raising the apparatus error required by §5.5 step 9 and §7.
