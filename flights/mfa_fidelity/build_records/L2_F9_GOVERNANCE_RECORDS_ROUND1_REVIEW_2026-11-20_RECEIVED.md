### Q3 — Master ruling: six attacks

**D1 (blocking): choice by commit grinding is understated.** A master is uniquely determined *given* a base commit, but the base commit is selectable. The record's claim “Nobody chooses the number” is therefore too strong. L1 or another committer can compute candidate masters for several prospective base commits, select one, and only push the selected commit. Requiring the selected base to be on remote `main` before authorization does not reveal the rejected local candidates. This is particularly material for design and held-out qualification, whose deterministic generated cases can be previewed once the master is known. The proposed rule provides auditability of a declared seed, **not** proof of an unselected draw, hidden draw, or unpreviewed qualification. A pushed base commit and a one-file authorization commit do not remedy local precomputation. Repair the security and scientific claims explicitly; ask Mike whether a checkable but previewable/selectable master is sufficient. If non-selection is required, option C as written is insufficient and a further independent commitment/entropy protocol is needed before runner build. Do not silently replace Mike's chosen option.

**D2 (blocking): design/held-out separation needs explicit protocol.** Step 6 seals the qualification declaration before step 7, which is helpful, but cannot alone ensure the design or held-out master was unexamined.
