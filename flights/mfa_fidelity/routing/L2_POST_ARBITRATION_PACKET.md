## 1. The proposition

**Under MFP, the MFA is interpreted as the mean-field projection of underlying stream-level dynamics. Its macro-valued quantities are artifacts of the averaging operation, not mechanisms the theory asserts.** **[v1.1: level-of-description wording per L2 — the tier is carried in the claim language itself; MFP is the registered interpretation, not a settled definitional fact.]**

Expanded: the stream-level picture — each agential action stream moving through the conditions it encounters, according to the (v,c,r) associated with that stream — is, under this interpretation, the underlying dynamics. The MFA arises from it by the standard mean-field operation: local encountered conditions replaced by population averages; configuration–neighborhood correlations set to zero; the configuration distribution collapsed to representative values. Every macro read in the MFA — above all Q's read of aggregate ρ — is what remains after this operation, because after averaging no local quantity survives to be read. The macro read is a notational necessity of the approximation, not a mechanism claim.

## 2. Tier and status

**Committed working interpretation** (L2: TIER APPROPRIATE; endorsed against both promotion and demotion). Not hard core: the hard core is stated at the MFA level and does not logically force a unique microscopic realization — an aggregate-feedback mechanism could conceivably produce the same MFA architecture, so entering MFP into the core now would retrospectively add a microfoundation the formal commitments did not establish. Not exploratory: MFP does genuine interpretive work across already-adopted elements (§3) and governs how the program currently understands the MFA–substrate relation. It binds interpretation and claim language; it remains revisable; its revision would not itself constitute rejection of the hard core; and it does not by itself decide any arbitration.

## 3. Standing evidence that the record already presupposes MFP

1. **V1 (variance ledger):** aggregate theorist-level Λ vs. local Λ, agents responding to neither as stimulus. L2: MFP is *compatible with* V1 rather than merely analogous — the same interpretive discipline, extended.
2. **Action ontology:** agents encounter only locally configured conditions; no equation admits a macro aggregate as agent input. MFP extends the same posture to the ground (Q), as registered interpretation rather than core commitment.
3. **Adopted MFA-parity analysis:** only coherent as an instrument if the MFA is a projection to be recovered. Under MFP the parity analysis is promoted to **the load-bearing bridge** between substrate findings and MFA claims.
4. **Genealogy:** Landau theory — the program's stated mathematical ground — is the canonical mean-field theory; Haken's order parameters carry the same projection relationship.

## 4. Consequences (registered with the proposition)

- **D2:** "fidelity" splits into fidelity-as-mimicry (the projected equation instantiated directly as substrate mechanism) and fidelity-as-recovery (stream-level reads implemented; MFA predictions tested for emergence under averaging). MFP removes the level-crossing inference "MFA-Q reads macro ρ, therefore the faithful substrate Q reads macro ρ." MFP does **not** rule D2. *(Historical note: Mike subsequently ruled D2 as (c) with local primary — see the D2 ruling record; the ruling cites MFP as input, consistent with the §5 fence.)*

The bridge is an evaluative instrument and receives full pre-seed outcome-hygiene treatment: **projection-level recovery targets are predeclared before any relevant data are seen.** MFA parity does not and cannot mean exact microscopic equivalence; absent predeclared targets, any mismatch could be dismissed as finite-size or spatial departure and any resemblance called recovery — the bridge would then be an escape hatch, which this clause forecloses. **The asymmetry binds:** a stream-level substrate that fails to recover the MFA behavior under the declared comparison is evidence against the proposed stream-level realization; MFP may not be invoked afterward as a reason the two were never supposed to agree.
