## The note (verbatim)

I'd like to approach things this way: the phenomenology of ee emergence, meaning the things that happen to bring it about, which may be highly variable from place to place and time to time (and I personally believe is highly variable, but that is an opinion, not an assumption and must be testable) must be kept paramount in the design. Ultimately, our toy city/region must test hypotheses about real world occurrences. Our substrate must be designed with this end in mind. It is essential that you understand my meaning. We are in no way ordained a conclusion, but the value we are trying to create is, does Psi exist and is there a regime II? The substrate, in order to achieve its true potential, needs to be able to test real world actions that are proposed to stimulate, maintain, or recover an ee. This possibility is truly exciting and it makes any effort and expense worthwhile. The ESO and, shall we call them affordable, layers will be critical, because they are the reason we are doing this, far beyond the MFA paper or this next flight. However, that future depends on this next flight in ways perhaps future flights won't. Do you see what I am asking for in both the recounting and memory of the full nuance of this chat?
