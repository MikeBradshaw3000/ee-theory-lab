## 5. Package and production grammar

- **Completeness:** an item that loads, validates, and satisfies its declared completeness predicate is complete, **including when its scientific content is adverse** (e.g., NOT_DESIGN-STABLE, REFERENCE-UNRESOLVED, a failed-but-valid stability result).
- **Two derived states:** *package complete* (every required item valid-complete) and *production ready* (every governance prerequisite meets its requirement, and no declared branch stop holds) are computed separately and never conflated.
- **Requirements by kind:** apparatus and qualification prerequisites require **passage**; scientific prerequisites require **valid completion**.
- **Branch stopping:** a scientific result stops the production branch **only if expressly declared** in `E1R_GOVERNANCE.json`. This version declares **none** (see §9 decision note).
