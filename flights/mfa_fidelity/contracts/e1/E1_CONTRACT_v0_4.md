## §5 Verdict function (rebuilt total; blockers 2, 3) and router (blocker 4)

**Definitions.** *Confident* labels: LEVEL-SUSTAINED (S) and LEVEL-NO (N). *Uncertain* labels: LEVEL-MIXED (M) and LEVEL-UNDISTINGUISHED (U). *Order violation:* any confident S at strictly lower m than any confident N. *Band* (when both confident phases exist and order is respected): the open interval (m of highest confident N, m of lowest confident S) — the candidate bracket [m₋, m₊] is its closure's endpoints.

**The verdict function — total by precedence; exactly one rule fires for every sequence in {S, N, M, U}^L:**

1. **Confident order violation → P1 FIRST TRANSITION NOT PRODUCED (order violation).** Covers resolved reversion and multiple resolved crossings — substantive adverse findings against the single-first-transition architecture.
2. **No confident N anywhere:** if the lowest level (hard boundary m = 0.15) is **confident S** → **NOT PRODUCED (no null-consistent phase)** — activation sustained even at the exhausted family's bottom, affirmatively established. Otherwise (bottom is M or U, or S first appears above an uncertain bottom) → **NOT DISTINGUISHED (bottom uncertainty)** — *uncertainty at the family boundary is never affirmative evidence of phase absence* (blocker 2's jurisprudence, adopted).
3. **No confident S anywhere:** if the highest level (m = 0.85) is **confident N** → **NOT PRODUCED (no sustained phase)**, affirmatively established at the exhausted top. Otherwise → **NOT DISTINGUISHED (top uncertainty).**
4. **Both confident phases present, order respected** — the band exists and is unique (uniqueness is a theorem of order-respect: all confident N below all confident S admits exactly one gap). Then:
   a. **Any M level outside the band → NOT DISTINGUISHED (off-boundary mixture).** MIXED is a real seed-composition finding; off the boundary it is structure the two-phase map cannot attribute at this resolution, and routing it to ND forecloses a false LOCATED (pessimistic-on-passing). *Explicit ruling per blocker 2: off-band MIXED is neither silently tolerated nor treated as adverse; it is a named instrument-resolution limit, and Mike may re-arbitrate it to adverse status in a successor if the audit shows it indicates order violation more often than composition noise.*
   b. **U levels outside the band:** tolerated up to **[PROPOSED]** 2, isolated and non-adjacent (allowance priced for hidden order violation in the §7 audit); more, or adjacent → **NOT DISTINGUISHED (off-boundary unresolved).**
   c. **Band interior:** M/U levels inside the band are boundary uncertainty. If the contiguous uncertain span at the boundary exceeds **[PROPOSED]** 3 levels after pass 2 → **NOT DISTINGUISHED (boundary unresolved).**
   d. **Otherwise → LOCATED THRESHOLD**, bracket [m₋, m₊] with [m₋³, m₊³]. Line-not-drama: no jump, slope, monotonicity, or separation criterion exists anywhere above.
(The v0.3 "boundary-less profile" category is dissolved by construction — order-respected dual-phase sequences always have the unique band; it classifies under 4a–4d.) **Totality and non-overlap are verified pre-freeze by exhaustive/property-based tests over generated sequences AND by the archetype table; the tests verify the written rules above — they create nothing** (L2's requirement).

**Pass-2 router (precedence-ordered; exactly one row executes; blocker 4):** inputs: pass-1 sequence; all inserted levels are **strictly new interior m values** (never duplicating pass-1 levels); same seed panel; nulls by rule-governed instantiation.
R1. Verdict-function rules 2 or 3 apply at pass 1 (a confident phase is absent) → **no pass 2** (census proceeds to the endpoint disposition).
R2. Confident order violation at pass 1 → **no pass 2.**
R3. Three or more disjoint N→S adjacency intervals → **no pass 2** (retained for totality; unreachable without order violation, which R2 precedes — noted, harmless).
R4. Exactly two such intervals → **4 + 4 evenly spaced strictly inside each** (retained on the same basis).
R5. The band exists and contains ≥ 1 uncertain level → **8 levels evenly spaced across the band's outer bounds** (strictly interior, non-duplicating).
R6. The band exists and is empty (adjacent confident N, S) → **8 levels evenly spaced strictly inside (m₋, m₊).**
R7. Otherwise → no pass 2.
Rows are precedence-ordered R1 > R2 > … > R7; R5/R6 are additionally mutually exclusive by band content. The final verdict function runs on the combined pass-1 + pass-2 sequence. Resource accounting and null instantiation follow the router.
