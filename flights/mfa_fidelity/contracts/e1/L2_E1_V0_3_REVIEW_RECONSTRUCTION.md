# L2 Re-Review — Contract E1 Draft v0.3

> **Archival re-export status:** Reconstructed from the operative v0.3-to-v0.4 integration record. The original wording is not available byte-for-byte; the nine binding findings are preserved.

## Overall verdict

**MAJOR ARCHITECTURE ACCEPTED; FREEZE MAY NOT PROCEED — NINE BLOCKERS REMAIN.**

v0.3 resolves the broad v0.2 defects. The remaining issues concern exact threshold-object jurisprudence, total routing, and the R1/evaluability reference machinery.

## 1. Null-object/statistic alignment

The run-tail null, level classifier, and profile boundary now have separate names, but the derivation between them is not fully specified. Each decision must identify the statistic, reference distribution, uncertainty object, and comparison operator. A quantile in run-density units cannot directly classify a seed fraction or bracket.

## 2. Endpoint adequacy and scientific jurisdiction

The low and high endpoint rules still do not completely distinguish:

- resolved absence of a phase within an adequate range;
- unresolved endpoint classification; and
- an endpoint outside the null/reference support.

Endpoint inadequacy produces NOT DISTINGUISHED. Adequate endpoints with no single transition produce the adverse P1 verdict. The router must make those branches disjoint.

## 3. Production verdict map must be total

Every profile of level labels `{N,S,U}` must terminate in exactly one production disposition. The draft still leaves ambiguity for broad U bands, multiple N→S transitions, S→N reversions, isolated classification islands, and profiles with no adjacent N/S pair.

Publish the router as a table or deterministic algorithm, not examples.

## 4. Pass-two router is not yet complete

The refinement rule must decide which coarse interval receives levels when multiple candidate intervals exist and what happens when the only separation contains U. It must also state whether refinement occurs after a scientifically adverse but resolved coarse pattern. No human inspection may select a more promising interval.

## 5. Threshold bracket and inherited object

The contract needs one normative threshold object in the control coordinate and explicit maps to theoretical and realized Lambda labels. The midpoint can be descriptive but is not the scientific object. E2 inherits the bracket/search region only; no E1 classifier tolerance travels.

## 6. R1-T1 equilibrium recovery

T1 must be conditional on the substrate run being sufficiently settled for an equilibrium comparison. A persistent nonstationary run may still support E1 while making T1 not evaluable. That is an R1 status, not a production-verdict change.

Freeze the equilibrium estimator, MFA equilibrium value, tolerance source, and the status grammar `RECOVERED / NOT RECOVERED / NOT EVALUABLE`.

## 7. R1-T2 threshold recovery

T2 currently merges two questions:

- whether the candidate MFA projection has the same transition structure; and
- whether its location agrees with the substrate bracket.

These must be separated. Structural nonrecovery cannot be hidden by a location metric, and location cannot be scored where the reference has no single threshold.

## 8. Declared departure surface

The near-threshold departure zone must be fixed in the control coordinate before production and must specify which R1 targets it qualifies. It cannot swallow the threshold-location test or expand after observing mismatch. Departures remain results; the zone changes attribution, not the production E1 verdict.

## 9. Evaluability morphology and error-rate decision

The synthetic audit must route all admissible profile archetypes through the actual run, level, endpoint, refinement, and verdict machinery. It must separately report:

- false scientific classification;
- false NOT DISTINGUISHED;
- nonmatching/undefined router output; and
- uncertainty of those estimates.

The proposed error caps must be judged on confidence bounds under a frozen replicate/analysis-seed rule, not on point estimates.

## Disposition

**FREEZE MAY NOT PROCEED.**

A focused v0.4 should finish the total production router and split the R1 threshold test into structural and location components.
