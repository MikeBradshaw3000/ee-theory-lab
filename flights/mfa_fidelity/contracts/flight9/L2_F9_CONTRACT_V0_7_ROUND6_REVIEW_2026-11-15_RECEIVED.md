\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{clip}(0.5+\sigma Z,0,1)]=0.5.
\]

At fixed σ, clipping is nondecreasing in the intercept, so for every c ≥ 0.60 and every σ ≥ 0 its mean is at least 0.5. This remains true pointwise when σ is σ_seed(c). Its population distance above θ = 0.20 is therefore at least 0.30. The disclosed minimum of approximately 0.584130467688 for the illustrated σ ≤ 0.35 is consistent with that broader bound. No added 0.06 condition is needed for this recipe. [C §6.2 saturated recipe; S7; direct symmetry and monotonicity argument.]
