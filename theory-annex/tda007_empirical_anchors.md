## 1. The instrument-facing half: what detection looks like

### 1.1 The signatures

Detection of Ψ is not "a number goes up." Under the committed architecture and the pitchfork working hypothesis, a detection has a specific composite signature:

**In Regime II:** Ψ fluctuates around zero — mean-zero, sign-incoherent across observation windows — no matter how high activation runs. A trace that wanders, changes sign, and pins to nothing.

**Across the second transition:** three fingerprints discriminate a genuine order-parameter onset from lookalikes. *Continuous onset* — the magnitude grows from zero as the μ(ρ) sign-change is crossed, with the characteristic square-root scaling near onset, no jump. *Spontaneous symmetry breaking* — two mirror branches exist, +Ψ and −Ψ, and which branch the system settles on is selected by fluctuation, not by anything in the terrain: identical conditions, different seeds, opposite signs. This is the signature that directional structure is emergent rather than imposed, and it is only expressible statistically — for a single realization the theory refuses to say which way it breaks. *Reversibility* — under the working hypothesis, walking the control back down returns Ψ continuously to zero; a jump with hysteresis (the system sticking on its branch as conditions retreat) would falsify the pitchfork form specifically, hitting the protective belt while the hard core survives.

**In Regime III:** the trace pins to one branch and holds it, variance dropping. Between regimes lie the delicate diagnostics — critical slowing, growing susceptibility — where pessimistic-on-passing bites hardest, because a windowed statistic can go nonzero for boring reasons (drift, transients, trends) that the probes must discriminate against.

### 1.1a What the sign means

The mirror branches invite an immediate question: what is −Ψ? Four layers of answer, from formal to novel.

*Formally,* sign is orientation and magnitude is coherence: |Ψ| measures how much directional structure exists; the sign labels which direction it points. The two branches are equally ordered configurations — nothing about the negative branch means less coherence. (The genealogical lake image carries this exactly: the quasi-particle can translate either way across the surface, and either way is equally a pattern.)

*Under the candidate operationalization* — Ψ built from the windowed slope of a participation measure — the sign becomes literal: +Ψ is coherent growth, −Ψ is coherent contraction, participation declining directionally and in an organized way.

*The novel claim this contains:* **coherent decline is not a return to Regime II.** A collapsing ecosystem can be highly ordered — the unwinding structured, everything moving together, downward. Disorder and decline are different axes, and the theory is nearly alone in being able to say so; the field's implicit picture treats losing coherence and declining as one thing, and Ψ < 0 says they are not. This gives the hollowing-out observation its formal address: an ecosystem decaying while its monuments stand is a −Ψ excursion, ordered all the way down.

*The symmetry is a fast-dynamics fact only, and Q breaks it* — which resolves the obvious objection that growth and decline are manifestly not equivalent states of a regional economy. The branches are mirrors of the cascade, not of the coupled system. The positive branch writes favorable conditions back into the bases, sustaining the very activation that holds μ positive: the joint attractor. The negative branch writes unfavorable conditions into the terrain, Λ falls, ρ falls, μ recrosses its sign change — coherent contraction extinguishes the substrate it lives on. So −Ψ is a real phase but a transient one at the Q timescale: an ordered path *out of* Regime III, not a place to stay. This predicts the observational asymmetry directly — long-lived coherent ecosystems are all growth-oriented not because the negative branch is impossible but because it is self-extinguishing, catchable only in the act, as an episode. The Waterloo anchor-collapse material (Section 2.2) is plausibly such an episode caught by scholars: the implicit question in the recycling-after-collapse work is whether the perturbation produced incoherence (a fall to Regime II) or an ordered negative excursion that re-pinned positive.

*And the symmetry is adjudicable.* Exact mirror symmetry belongs to the pitchfork working hypothesis. An intrinsic bias toward the growth direction — a linear tilt in the potential — would make the bifurcation imperfect: one branch preferred, onset smoothed, no true fluctuation-selection. Seed-ensemble branch statistics therefore discriminate: an even split under symmetric conditions produces evidence for the committed form; systematic positive preference produces evidence of a bias term and returns the pitchfork commitment for revision — protective belt, not hard core.

### 1.2 Units, and why the question dissolves

The architecture assigns Ψ no units; it assigns Ψ a *role* — zero here, nonzero there, continuous onset, broken symmetry. Units attach only when an operationalization fixes what Ψ is computed from; under a windowed-slope construction the raw quantity carries dimensions of participation-change per tick, but order parameters are conventionally reported normalized and dimensionless, and rightly so here: what matters is Ψ's value relative to its own onset scaling, not its absolute magnitude. The one genuinely architectural quantity hiding in the units question is the **window length** — not a tuning knob but a declaration of the timescale on which Ψ is defined, the same epistemic species as the participation window W (participation document, D5).

### 1.3 The translation key

In EE terms, the second transition is *participation becoming sufficient for directional structure*. Ψ nonzero is that sentence rendered as a number. This is the sentence the literature-facing half searches for in other scholars' vocabulary.

## 2. The literature-facing half: candidate cases

The literature does not use the theory's vocabulary, so the search targets *descriptions*: for Regime II, substantial entrepreneurial action coexisting with persistent scholarly characterization of the place as fragmented, disconnected, or "not yet an ecosystem" (high ρ, Ψ ≈ 0 in translation); for Regime III, not merely high activity but documented *self-maintenance* — the ecosystem reproducing its own conditions, which in the theory's terms is the joint-attractor reading: Ψ ≠ 0 sustained with Q visibly recycling people, capital, and know-how back into the bases. Every mapping below is a candidate, not a confirmation.

### 2.1 Regime II candidates

**Calgary (Spigel 2017, ETP).** Weak ties between the ecosystem's attributes; entrepreneurs spending little time with other entrepreneurs, their networks oriented toward the oil-and-gas industry rather than the entrepreneurial community; low trust impeding community formation. High action, no collective order.

**Aberdeen (Spigel & Harrison 2018, SEJ)** — the closest the field has come to naming Regime II as a category. Their "irrigated ecosystem" type: energy-driven economies with high startup rates driven by industry opportunity, where competition and rivalry impede mutual learning and weak networks leave the ecosystem without resiliency. That is activated-but-incoherent in their vocabulary, which makes the **"irrigated" category the anchor citation for the manuscript's Regime II positioning**: it shows the field already observed the phenomenon and lacked only the architecture to place it.

**St. Louis (Motoyama & Knowlton 2014, 2017)** — the methodological companion rather than just a case. Arguing that aggregated conceptualizations obscure how elements connect, they analyzed connections at multiple layers — among entrepreneurs, among support organizations, and between the two — in a place with abundant elements where the connections themselves were the open question. Their layered-connection method is essentially a Ψ-measurement instrument built without the concept, which makes it a lead for the observable-design work, not only a citation.

**Phoenix (Mack & Mayer 2016, Urban Studies)** — the temporal candidate. Their four-stage model asks why places remain trapped in a phase; Malecki's reading — narrow scope, possible decline, "partial at best" — is a description a two-transition architecture gives an address to.

**As a class:** Brown & Mason's (2017) embryonic/scale-up typology conflates low-ρ and low-Ψ cases in its "embryonic" category — exactly the distinction Regime II makes and their typology cannot. The conflation is itself evidence that the distinction is needed.

### 2.2 Regime III candidates

**Silicon Valley** — the paradigm, documented before the EE vocabulary existed: Bahrami & Evans (1995) describing a mutually supportive spiral of entrepreneurship and innovation with "flexible re-cycling" of old firms into new. "Spiral" is a practitioner-observed description of the fast-order/slow-terrain loop; resources injected back into the pool that funds further ventures is Q writing to the bases, in their vocabulary.

**Waterloo** — the strongest candidate *because it is paired* (Section 3): dense networks, skills and investor access, and — stated explicitly in the literature — high entrepreneurial activity reproducing and reinforcing the region's pre-existing entrepreneurial culture. Spigel & Vinodrai's follow-up on recycling after the anchor-firm collapse (BlackBerry) functions as a **natural perturbation experiment on a coherent state** — a large disturbance to a putative Ψ ≠ 0 configuration with the response documented, which bears on the reversibility working hypothesis and deserves its own close read at the measurement-theory horizon.

**Boulder** (Feld's home case; Neck et al. 2004 placing it with San Diego and Waterloo as regions where entrepreneurial culture is sustained by civic-mindedness) and **San Diego** (Walshok's CONNECT literature as the deeper vein).

**Kyoto (Aoyama 2009) and Washington DC (Feldman 2001)** — pre-vocabulary cases of interlocking, historically produced, place-based conditions for long-term entrepreneurial success. **Feldman's DC study matters doubly:** she documents an ecosystem forming where one had not existed — the literature's best candidate observation of *nucleation*, connecting directly to the open nucleation element and the district material (TDA-001 §3).

**Tel Aviv and Stockholm** — present in comparative work alongside Silicon Valley, with less depth on the self-maintenance mechanism specifically; second-tier anchors.

## 3. The paired-contrast logic: the central methodological asset

The lists matter less than their structure. **Waterloo/Calgary** and **Silicon Valley/Aberdeen** are same-method, same-scholar-tradition contrasts in which activity is comparable and the described difference is precisely the presence or absence of collective order. This is as close as observational literature comes to a controlled comparison: scholar and method held fixed, activation roughly matched, coherence the residual difference. These pairs — not the individual cases — are the natural empirical anchors for the manuscript's Regime II/III distinction, because a skeptic can dismiss any single city's description as idiosyncratic, but a within-scholar contrast where the *only* consistent difference is the one the theory names is a different kind of evidence.

The pairing also embodies the positioning frame. Nothing here supersedes Spigel, Motoyama, Mack & Mayer, or Feldman; each receives a precise address within the architecture — irrigated ecosystems at (ρ high, Ψ ≈ 0); embryonic typologies at the conflation the regime structure resolves; recycling literatures at the Q channel; Feldman's DC at nucleation. Situating, never replacing, with the addresses now specific enough to cite.

## 4. The caveats, elevated to method

Four disciplines govern any use of this material, and they are the document's real content as much as the lists are:

**Correlate, not measurement.** "Weak networks" is not the order parameter; network density is a *plausible correlate* of Ψ whose mapping the observable-design work must earn. Likewise "recycling documented" is a candidate correlate of Ψ ≠ 0 *plus active Q* — and note it conflates the two: recycling is a slow Q-channel process, coherence a fast order parameter; the literature observes them fused, which is consistent with the joint-attractor reading but does not discriminate for it. Constructing the actual mappings is an instance of the empirical normalization problem and belongs to the measurement-theory horizon.

**Pessimistic-on-passing.** Every listed city could be described as it is for reasons other than a Ψ transition — sheer scale, industry composition, capital depth. The candidate status is not modesty; it is the rule applied.

**The industry-channeling confound, used correctly.** For Calgary and Aberdeen one can argue Λ itself is configured differently (opportunity channeled through one industry) rather than coherence failing downstream. This is not a weakness to hide but the *alternative explanation a Regime II empirical claim must be designed to discriminate against* — the confound tells the future empirical work what its controls must be.

## 5. The two halves, joined

The instrument-facing signatures and the literature-facing candidates are the same question asked of two archives. The substrate asks: does a lattice of action-taking agents produce a quantity that sits at zero through high activation and then pins to a fluctuation-selected branch with continuous onset? The literature asks: have careful observers, without the vocabulary, repeatedly described places where activation is high and directional order absent, and other places where order visibly sustains itself? A program in which both answers come back affirmative — the model producing the regime structure, the field having unknowingly catalogued it — is in the strongest position a generative theory can occupy short of direct measurement. And the joint reading has a built-in honesty check: if the substrate *fails* to produce Regime II, the literature's irrigated ecosystems don't vanish — they become the anchor observations for whatever mathematics the seminal core seeks next. The anchors are anchored to the phenomenon, not to the formalization; that is what makes them safe to build on.
